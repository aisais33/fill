2. What We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data when you interact with our website or services:

  • Identity & contact data — full name, email address, telephone number, company or organisation name.
  • Enquiry data — number of seats, programme of interest, preferred timeline, and any additional notes you provide through our contact or quote-request forms.
  • Technical data — IP address (anonymised where analytics are used), browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps.

We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 16. Our services are directed at business professionals and organisations seeking systems-administration training.

3. Legal Basis & Purpose

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual necessity — Processing your name, contact details, and enquiry information is necessary so that we can prepare and deliver a training proposal or quotation you have requested. Without this data we would be unable to respond to your enquiry.
  • Legitimate interest — We use anonymised analytics data to understand how visitors use our website, identify technical issues, and improve the user experience. We have conducted a balancing test and concluded that this processing does not override your rights or freedoms.
  • Consent — Where we deploy marketing or advertising cookies, we do so only after you have given clear, affirmative consent through our cookie banner. You may withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your cookie preferences or contacting us directly.

4. How We Use Data

Personal data you provide is used to:

  • Respond to training enquiries and prepare proposals.
  • Administer bookings, invoicing, and related correspondence.
  • Send transactional communications (confirmations, schedule changes, receipts).
  • Analyse aggregated, anonymised website-usage patterns to improve content and functionality.
  • Comply with legal obligations, including tax and accounting requirements.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each of which operates only after you have provided the appropriate consent via our cookie banner:

  • Google Analytics — used for website-traffic analysis. IP anonymisation is enabled so that your full IP address is never stored. Data is processed in accordance with Google's data-processing terms.
  • Google Ads — used to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns and to present relevant advertisements. Conversion data is collected only when you have consented to marketing cookies.
  • Google Tag Manager (GTM) — used to manage the deployment of the analytics and advertising tags listed above. GTM itself does not collect personal data but facilitates the loading of scripts that may do so, subject to your consent choices.

We do not share personal data from our enquiry forms with any third-party service. Form data is stored securely on our own systems and accessed only by authorised staff.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies, which fall into three categories:

  • Necessary cookies — required for the basic operation of the website (e.g., remembering your cookie-consent preference). These do not require consent and cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — used by Google Analytics to collect anonymised information about how visitors navigate our site. These are set only after you consent.
  • Marketing cookies — used by Google Ads to track advertising performance and serve relevant advertisements. These are set only after you consent.

You can manage or withdraw your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site, or by contacting us.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Form submissions — retained for up to 24 months from the date of submission, after which they are securely deleted unless a continuing business relationship exists.
  • Analytics data — retained for up to 14 months, in line with standard analytics data-retention settings. This data is anonymised and cannot be linked back to an identifiable individual.

Where we are required by law to retain records for longer (for example, for tax purposes), we will do so in accordance with applicable legislation.

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access — You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — You may ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — You may request that we delete your personal data where there is no compelling reason for us to continue processing it.
  • Right to restrict processing — You may ask us to suspend processing of your data in certain circumstances (for example, while we verify its accuracy).
  • Right to data portability — Where processing is based on consent or contractual necessity and carried out by automated means, you may request your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — You may object to processing based on legitimate interest. We will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.
  • Right to withdraw consent — Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — If you are dissatisfied with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by telephone on 0303 123 1113.

9. International Transfers

Some of the third-party services described in Section 5 may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where such transfers occur, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the Information Commissioner's Office, or reliance on an adequacy decision recognised under UK data-protection law. These measures are designed to ensure that your personal data receives a level of protection equivalent to that afforded within the United Kingdom.

10. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the rights listed in Section 8, please contact us by email at enquiries@fargatesystems.co.uk or by post at the address given in Section 1. We will respond to your request within one calendar month. In exceptional circumstances, where the request is particularly complex or we receive a large number of requests, we may extend this period by a further two months and will notify you accordingly.

We may ask you to verify your identity before processing your request to ensure that personal data is not disclosed to an unauthorised person.

11. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include, but are not limited to:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers.
  • Role-based access controls limiting access to personal data to authorised personnel who require it for their duties.
  • Regular reviews of our data-processing practices and security procedures.

Whilst we strive to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is entirely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to maintaining best-practice standards.
