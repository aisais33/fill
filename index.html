Case study

Financial services firm — Edinburgh region — 12-person IT team

Challenge

Mean time to restore service after an incident was sitting at 53 minutes. Two senior engineers handled nearly every escalation — the rest of the team lacked confidence to act without approval. Knowledge was concentrated, not distributed.

What we did

Ran 8 engineers through the Professional tier over 10 weeks. Built custom lab scenarios mirroring their actual environment — same stack, same failure modes. Added an incident response drill module that they hadn't asked for but clearly needed.

Result

MTTR dropped to 22 minutes within three months. Four junior engineers now handle tier-2 escalations independently. The two seniors? They actually took holiday for the first time in two years. Not every engagement produces numbers this clean — but this one did.

FAQ

How quickly can we start?
Essential and Professional cohorts start twice monthly. Enterprise engagements typically need 2–3 weeks for the initial skills audit and curriculum design before training begins.
Can you deliver on-site?
Yes — for Enterprise tier. Anywhere in the UK. Travel and accommodation are included in the quote. No separate invoice for expenses.
What's the refund policy?
Full refund within 14 days of enrolment if you haven't started the lab modules. After that, no refunds — but you can transfer your place to a colleague.
Do you offer CPD accreditation?
Yes. All programmes are CPD-accredited. Hours are logged and a certificate issued upon completion — useful for annual reviews and L&D records.
Can our employer pay by invoice?
Yes. NET 30 terms for UK corporate clients. Purchase orders accepted. We can provide a summary document for your procurement or L&D team.
